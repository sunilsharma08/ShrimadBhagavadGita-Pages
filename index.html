Bhagavad Gita – Insights
(Shrimad Bhagavad Gita)
View on App Store
Discover the timeless wisdom of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita in a simple and accessible way. This app brings the Gita to your fingertips in Sanskrit, Hindi, and English, with clear translations and explanations that stay true to the original meaning.

The app is designed to be approachable for everyone — whether you are exploring the Gita for the first time or revisiting it for daily reflection. It offers multiple customisation options, including themes, font size, and reading preferences.

Simple, customisable, and designed to help you connect with the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita every day.
